Crumb

On a recipe page, the extension asks “Read this recipe in Crumb?”. Say yes and your Crumb opens it, ready to add.

Or open your Crumb while signed in and choose “Use this Crumb”.

Off, the toolbar button still reads the page you're on.

    The extension looks for recipe data on the pages you open, in your browser. It sends nothing anywhere until you choose to read a page, and then only that page's address, to your Crumb.
